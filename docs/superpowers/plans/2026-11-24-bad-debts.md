# Bad Debts: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A long-overdue sales invoice asks to be written off; the write-off settles it, books the bad debt and its
VAT relief in the write-off's quarter, and a later payment from that customer is a recovery.

**Architecture:** A new document type `write_off` (a row dated the write-off, tied to its invoice by `writes_off`)
that checks books with its VAT relief, posting books against Debtors, and matching lets settle its invoice. Matching
marks a later bank line from the customer as a recovery (`recovers`), which posting books against Bad Debts with
its VAT to Sales VAT. Questions ask about overdue invoices and add the write-off row from the answer.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, pydantic, pytest; the question card in Next/React (TypeScript, node:test).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-bad-debts-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No model calls. Targeted tests only: `LEDGERSYNC_TEST_MONGODB_URI=mongodb://localhost:27017 .venv/bin/python -m pytest <files> -q`, never the whole `tests/` (about 55 minutes), never with `-W error`.
- Accounts: 8100 Bad Debts, 1100 Debtors, 2201 Purchase VAT (relief), 2200 Sales VAT (recovery's VAT).
- Due date: invoice date + 30 days; the question asks once six calendar months past it, by the earlier of today
  and the year's end.
- Relief only when the client is VAT registered on the invoice's date and the invoice booked VAT.
- Commits only after the user's yes, each ending `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. An invoice part-paid before the write-off: only what is still owed is written off, its VAT share pro rata.
2. A recovery bigger than what was written off: only up to the write-off is a recovery; the rest is matched as today.
3. A write-off row removed: the invoice is owed again and its question comes back.
4. A client not VAT registered: no relief, the whole gross to Bad Debts.
5. A write-off dated outside the year: refused.
