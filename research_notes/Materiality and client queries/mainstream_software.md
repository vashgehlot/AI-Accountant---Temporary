# How mainstream accounting, receipt-capture and expense software decides auto-code vs flag vs ask

Scope: Xero (bank rules, memory, JAX, Hubdoc), QuickBooks Online (AI bank feeds, accounting agent, client requests), Sage (Copilot, AutoEntry), Dext Prepare / Precision, AutoEntry, Pleo, Expensify, Ramp, Brex, Spendesk, SAP Concur (Verify, Detect). Researched November 2026. Most sources are vendor help centres (documented behaviour) and vendor marketing (claims). Marketing figures are labelled as vendor claims. A summarising search tool was used for some findings, so short quotes below may be paraphrased.

## Q1. Thresholds: vendor-fixed or customer-set? Defaults?

### Takeaway
Almost every monetary threshold is customer-set. Vendors ship the rule *slots* (receipt-required-over-X, flag-over-Y, per-attendee limits, auto-approve-under-Z) but, in the docs found, leave the amounts blank. The one documented numeric default is Expensify's 5% random-audit rate. The vendor-fixed elements are categorical rather than monetary: hard-blocked merchant types, never-auto-publish document types, and "essential field missing" blockers.

### Cited Findings
**Expensify (New Expensify workspace rules, documented)**
- The General tab has "Flag expenses older than [N days]", "Flag expenses above amount" (category rules can override it), "Require receipts / itemized receipts above set amounts", required category/tag, and a flag on receipt line items for restricted purchases (alcohol, gambling, tobacco). The help page lists no numeric defaults. — [Expensify Help: Workspace Rules](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules)
- The Field requirements tab works per category: require a description, attendees, a receipt or an itemized receipt, or waive the receipt requirement. The Flag for review tab works per category: it alerts the approver when a single expense *or the daily category total on a report* exceeds an amount the admin sets. Category rules take priority over General rules. — [Expensify Help: Workspace Rules](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules)
- Attendee tracking is on every expense and computes per-person cost. Limits apply per person, so an expense that exceeds the limit for any attendee raises a violation. — [Expensify Help: Workspace Rules](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules); [Expensify Classic: Attendee Tracking (mirror)](https://6b5c754b.helpdot.pages.dev/articles/expensify-classic/reports/Attendee-Tracking)
- Vendor-fixed: Expensify Card always declines adult services, ATMs, gambling and money transfers, and admins cannot edit this. SmartScan cannot be disabled on paid workspaces. — [Expensify Help: Workspace Rules](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules)
- The only numeric default found: the random audit of *compliant* reports ("Randomly route reports for manual approval") defaults to 5%, about 1 in 20. — [Expensify: Set a random report audit schedule](https://help.expensify.com/articles/expensify-classic/reports/Set-a-random-report-audit-schedule)
- Report-level rules under Workflows > Advanced: prevent self-approval, auto-approve compliant reports, auto-pay approved reports. The page names them but gives no default thresholds. — [Expensify Help: Workspace Rules](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules)

**Ramp (documented)**
- Policy Agent checks against the customer's own "agent-facing policy document". Ramp converts the customer's existing written policy into this structured form, and thresholds, limits and exceptions come from it. USD limits are checked against the converted USD value of non-USD spend. Reference data such as GSA per-diem rates can be used. — [Ramp Support: Policy Agent Overview](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/44072387128979-Policy-Agent-Overview)
- Auto-approval is set by workflow, for example auto-approve under an amount or for certain departments or merchants. — [Ramp Support: Use Policy Agent for approvals](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/47618318137875-Use-Policy-Agent-for-approvals)
- Submission requirements (receipt, memo) and card auto-lock for overdue items are admin-configured. The admin picks the deadline and can exclude people or requirement types. — [Ramp Support: Missing-items auto-locks](https://support.ramp.com/missing-items-auto-locks); [Ramp Support: Automate receipts and expense requirements](https://support.ramp.com/automate-receipts-and-expense-requirements)

**Dext / AutoEntry / Hubdoc (documented)**
- Dext auto-publish is set per supplier/customer, per payment method, or for all items. It applies only to items submitted after the rule exists. Priority order: payment-method rules and approval workflows, then supplier rules, then "Auto-publish all". Vendor-fixed blockers: essential fields not extracted, item flagged as suspicious, required fields (such as Category) not completed, and cost-item exception types (some receipts, invoices, supplier statements, as configured) that never auto-publish. Dext AI Assist "guidance" rules can combine conditions such as supplier *and amount*, which puts the amount threshold in the customer's hands. — [Dext Help: How to use auto-publish in Dext](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/377051-how-to-use-auto-publish-in-dext)
- AutoEntry auto-publish is turned on per supplier once a nominal and VAT code are saved for that supplier. AutoEntry advises against it for Sage 50 / QuickBooks Desktop. — [AutoEntry Help: Auto-publish](https://intercom.help/autoentry/en/articles/1313041-auto-publish)
- Hubdoc auto-sync is set per supplier. Documents detected as credit notes never auto-publish to QuickBooks and need manual review. — [Hubdoc: Customise configurations by supplier](https://support.hubdoc.com/hc/en-us/articles/16568850613261); [Hubdoc: Publish to QuickBooks Online](https://support.hubdoc.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001288363)

**Brex / Pleo / Concur**
- Brex: a "List attendees" setting prompts the spender for extra information on transactions in the Meals category. Admins configure it; it is not a default. — [Brex Support: Brex Assistant](https://brex.com/support/brex-assistant)
- Pleo AI expense review checks expenses against the company's own "spending guidelines" (category, amount, receipt, notes). — [Pleo Help: AI expense review](https://help.pleo.io/en/support/solutions/articles/103000370241-ai-expense-review) (the page returned 403 when fetched; content taken from the search snippet)
- SAP Concur community threads show receipt requirements and per-attendee amount limits configured per policy group by the customer admin. These are user forum posts, not official docs. — [Concur Community: Policy group receipt requirements](https://community.concur.com/t5/Concur-Expense-Forum/Policy-group-receipt-requirements/m-p/114677); [Concur Community: link number of attendees to amount limit](https://community.concur.com/t5/Concur-Expense/How-to-link-number-of-attendees-to-amount-limit-of-certain/m-p/44582)

### Inferences
- The industry pattern is "vendor supplies the rule types, customer supplies the numbers." No product found publishes a recommended default such as "receipt over £25" in its help docs. Any sensible default would be our own design choice, or would come from tax-authority rules such as the US IRS $75 receipt rule, which was not researched here.
- Rules exist at three levels: a global rule, per-category overrides (Expensify), and per-supplier or per-payment-method overrides (Dext). There is an explicit precedence order, with the most specific rule winning (Expensify: category over general; Dext: payment method over supplier over global).
- Per-attendee cost is a first-class field in Expensify (computed automatically). Ramp has the agent read attendees; Brex fills them from calendars.

### Gaps
- No vendor-published default monetary thresholds found for Expensify, Ramp, Pleo, Brex, Spendesk or Concur.
- Expensify per-diem rules were not researched in depth; only "Require company cards" flagging of per diem/mileage was found.
- Concur's official documentation on audit-rule configuration was not retrieved; only community posts.

## Q2. Confidence scores: used to decide auto-post vs review? Exposed to users?

### Takeaway
Confidence gating is common in the ledgers. Xero JAX auto-reconciles only on "high confidence", and QBO shows green/blue badges and hides low-confidence suggestions. Numeric confidence scores are not exposed anywhere found. Vendors show either a coarse tier (QBO badges), a three-way verdict (Ramp approve / review / reject; Pleo OK / flagged / Unknown; Concur Detect low / medium / high risk), or nothing (Xero shows only what was auto-reconciled).

### Cited Findings
**Xero JAX auto bank reconciliation (beta)**
- JAX auto-reconciles only when it has "high confidence". Otherwise it still suggests a match or category but leaves the line for manual reconciliation. Xero doesn't define how the threshold is calculated. — [Xero Blog: Automatic bank reconciliation with JAX (beta), 20 Nov 2025](https://blog.xero.com/product-updates/automatic-bank-reconciliation-jax-beta/); [Xero campaign page](https://www.xero.com/uk/campaign/auto-bank-reconciliation/)
- No per-transaction confidence score is shown. Auto-reconciled lines go to a new "Reconciled" page where users can review or adjust them, and automation is toggled per bank account. — [Xero Blog](https://blog.xero.com/product-updates/automatic-bank-reconciliation-jax-beta/)
- It uses four methods: Match, Rule, Memory and Prediction. — [Xero campaign page](https://www.xero.com/campaign/auto-bank-reconciliation/); [CFOtech](https://cfotech.com.au/story/xero-rolls-out-ai-bank-reconciliation-with-jax-tool)
- Vendor claims: the goal is more than 80% of lines auto-reconciled in real time. The campaign page claims over 60% auto-reconciled at about 96% accuracy, where accuracy means the share of auto-reconciled lines not later corrected by users (rolling 3-month average, as stated in the footnote). — [Xero campaign page](https://www.xero.com/id/campaign/auto-bank-reconciliation/)
- Availability: global beta from the week of 20 Nov 2025. Plans: Grow (AU/NZ/UK), Growing (US), Standard (global) and above; lower tiers via an "AI Automate" add-on (per the campaign page). — [Xero Blog](https://blog.xero.com/product-updates/automatic-bank-reconciliation-jax-beta/); [Xero campaign page](https://www.xero.com/campaign/auto-bank-reconciliation/)

**QuickBooks Online (AI bank transactions page)**
- Each match suggestion carries a confidence badge. Green means high (strong evidence, so review the other fields and post). Blue means medium (ambiguous: a new payee, several candidates, or thin history, so confirm first). *Low-confidence suggestions are hidden* and the user must use "Find match". — [QuickBooks Help: AI suggestions to match and categorise bank transactions](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-global/help-article/bank-transactions/ai-suggestions-help-match-categorise-bank/L8FHOh4AD_ROW_en)
- Evidence behind the badge: exact amount match, payee alignment, a single clear candidate, date proximity, how often the user has accepted this kind of match, and direction (money in or out). In a new company file, even a clean match may not show green until the user has accepted that match type several times. — [QuickBooks Help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-global/help-article/bank-transactions/ai-suggestions-help-match-categorise-bank/L8FHOh4AD_ROW_en)
- Categories: a "Top suggestion" is pre-filled only when one category clearly stands out. Otherwise "Consider" categories appear behind an icon and are not pre-filled. If the supplier or customer can't be identified, no suggestion is shown. An orange "potential match" warning replaces a category suggestion when history shows the user usually *matches* this kind of transaction. — [QuickBooks Help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-global/help-article/bank-transactions/ai-suggestions-help-match-categorise-bank/L8FHOh4AD_ROW_en)
- Accounting agent: suggestions "must be approved by you or your Advisor before any transaction can be posted", so nothing auto-posts. Each suggestion includes an explanation. AI bank feeds are opt-in for now; AI output is English-only at launch. — [QuickBooks: Accounting agent product update](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/global/resources/product-update/quickbooks-accounting-agent/)
- A search summary described a "Ready to post" grouping of transactions where Accounting AI is confident. The fetched help article does not use that phrase, so treat it as unconfirmed. — [QuickBooks Help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-global/help-article/bank-transactions/ai-suggestions-help-match-categorise-bank/L8FHOh4AD_ROW_en)

**Ramp Policy Agent**
- There are three verdicts. "Approval recommended" means clearly compliant. "Requires review" means the agent is uncertain, context is missing, or policy requires a human. "Rejection recommended" means a clear violation. No numeric confidence is shown; when the agent can't decide confidently it chooses Requires review rather than guessing. Each verdict shows its rationale and the cited policy text. — [Ramp Support: Policy Agent Overview](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/44072387128979-Policy-Agent-Overview)
- Vendor claim: about 85% fewer manual reviews at 99%+ accuracy. — [Ramp Support: Policy Agent Overview](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/44072387128979-Policy-Agent-Overview)
- Available on Ramp Plus. — [Ramp Support: Policy Agent Overview](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/44072387128979-Policy-Agent-Overview)

**Pleo AI expense review**
- Expenses within guidelines are marked "OK" and can be bulk-approved. Flagged ones go to admins, and items the AI can't assess are marked "Unknown" for manual review. Beyond plan only. — [Pleo Help: AI expense review](https://help.pleo.io/en/support/solutions/articles/103000370241-ai-expense-review); [Pleo Help: Finance automation](https://pleohelp.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/103000398332-finance-automation-with-pleo) (search snippets; the page returned 403 when fetched)

**Spendesk**
- An AI check compares the receipt's supplier name, date and amount with the payable. Spendesk says a failed check "does not mean that it is invalid" and that the employee should be contacted for the correct receipt. — [Spendesk Help: Anomaly and fraud checks on receipts in Prepare](https://helpcenter.spendesk.com/en/articles/11739382-anomaly-and-fraud-checks-on-receipts-in-prepare)
- Customer case study (vendor-published): 85–95% of submissions get "reliable AI suggestions". — [Spendesk: Orbem case study](https://www.spendesk.com/customers/orbem/)

**SAP Concur Detect by Oversight**
- Risk findings are used to "prioritize exceptions based on confidence and potential impact". Reports are classed by risk level, and customers can set low-risk reports to auto-approve. — [Concur: Detect by Oversight](https://www.concur.com.au/detect-expense-fraud); [Concur Community: How are users liking Concur Detect](https://community.concur.com/t5/Additional-Products-Forum/How-are-users-liking-Concur-Detect/m-p/6546)

### Inferences
- Three output designs recur. (a) A binary gate (auto vs leave for human, as in Xero JAX and Dext auto-publish). (b) A three-way verdict with an explicit "uncertain" bucket (Ramp, Pleo, Concur Detect). (c) Graded suggestion display (QBO green / blue / hidden). The "uncertain → human" bucket appears in every AI reviewer studied.
- Instead of numbers, vendors show the *reasons* (QBO's evidence list, Ramp's cited policy text, QBO agent explanations). That is consistent with keeping a numeric confidence internal and exposing tiers plus rationale.
- QBO makes confidence depend on the user's own acceptance history: a new file starts with nothing green. This is a cold-start safeguard worth copying.
- Xero measures accuracy as "lines not later corrected by users", a practical metric we could reuse.

### Gaps
- No vendor publishes its numeric confidence threshold.
- Sage Copilot's approach to confidence and auto-posting in Sage Accounting was not documented in what was found. A Sage community thread shows Copilot "categorised and completed" transactions, which then duplicated manual entries. — [Sage Community Hub thread](https://communityhub.sage.com/gb/sage-sole-trader/f/general-discussion-sbc-sole-trader/274951/copilot-has-categorised-and-completed-numerous-transactions)

## Q3. Anomaly detection relative to history (unusual amount, duplicates, out-of-pattern)

### Takeaway
Duplicate detection is near-universal and documented: Expensify, Dext (documents and Precision transactions), Concur Verify, and Sage's warnings. "Unusual vs history" detection is documented in QBO's accounting agent (Plus/Advanced), Concur Detect (prior behaviour, repeat offenders), Dext Precision ("irregular transactions based on contact history", per marketing), Sage Intacct (GL outlier detection), and Ramp (marketed, with one documented case: AI spend spikes). The methods are not published.

### Cited Findings
- **QBO accounting agent (Plus and Advanced only):** flags unusual transactions, such as "an invoice much higher than normal" or a payment that doesn't fit typical patterns. It also flags potential discrepancies in P&L and Balance Sheet reports, with guidance on investigating each. Duplicate detection isn't mentioned on that page. — [QuickBooks: Accounting agent](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/global/resources/product-update/quickbooks-accounting-agent/)
- Intuit says its accounting analysis is a supplemental review, "not a substitute for professional judgment". — [CPA Practice Advisor: Overview of Intuit AI in QBO 2026](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/?p=193399)
- **Expensify:** duplicate detection runs before submission and labels items "Potential duplicate" (Collect and Control plans). — [Expensify Help: Duplicate detection](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/reports-and-expenses/Duplicate-Detection)
- **Dext Precision:** the duplicate-transactions check compares contact, date and value across invoices and bank transactions. Results show as cards, and dismissing a match stops it being counted. — [Dext Help: Using the duplicate transactions check](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/276847-using-the-duplicate-transactions-check)
- **Dext Prepare:** duplicate bills and receipts handling has Automatic, Review and Off modes, for Costs only. — [Dext Help: How does Dext handle duplicate documents](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/216124-how-does-dext-handle-duplicate-documents)
- **Dext Precision:** marketed as identifying "mis-coded invoices and irregular transactions based on contact history". VAT return checks identify high-value transactions. The thresholds behind "irregular" were not found. — [Dext Help: duplicate transactions](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/duplicate-transactions) (the claim surfaced via a search summary of Dext's Xero listing; method unverified)
- **SAP Concur Verify:** AI reviews 100% of reports for duplicates (including duplicate receipts *across employees*), out-of-policy spend and incorrect dates. — [Concur: Verify](https://www.concur.com/verify); [SAP News: Making an impact with Verify (2023)](https://news.sap.com/2023/07/making-an-impact-with-verify/)
- Verify statistics (SAP-published): about one-third of reports are flagged per month on average. Separately, 97% of 2.8M reports from Jan 2022 to Jun 2023 were "audited automatically", with 3% sent to a human. The two figures measure different things, and SAP's own posts sit in tension with each other. — [SAP News (2023)](https://news.sap.com/2023/07/making-an-impact-with-verify/); [SAP: Automate expense audits](https://www.sap.com/use-cases/automate-expense-audits)
- **Concur Detect by Oversight:** multivariate analysis of every expense, looking at "prior behavior" and going "beyond threshold-based exceptions"; flags repeat offenders; works before and after reimbursement. — [Concur: Detect by Oversight](https://www.concur.com.au/detect-expense-fraud); [Concur UK brochure](https://www.concur.co.uk/resource-centre/brochures/concur-detect-by-oversight)
- **Sage Intacct:** GL outlier detection is said to review more than 15 million transactions per week (vendor claim). It is not documented for Sage Accounting bank feeds. — [Sage: AI accounting software](https://sage.com/en-gb/accounting-software/ai/); [Techfinitive: Sage expands Intacct with AI finance controls](https://www.techfinitive.com/sage-expands-intacct-with-ai-powered-finance-controls/)
- **Ramp:** marketing claims "real-time anomaly detection on every swipe", including duplicate vendors and unusual amounts. The documented example is the "AI spend spikes" check, which compares each employee's daily AI-tool spend with their own norm and with peers. Policy Agent may also use recent expense history as context. — [Ramp: Expense management](https://ramp.com/expense-management); [Ramp Support: AI spend spikes](https://support.ramp.com/ai-spend-spikes); [Ramp Support: Policy Agent Overview](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/44072387128979-Policy-Agent-Overview)
- **Spendesk:** "anomaly and fraud checks" on receipts compare receipt fields with the payable. — [Spendesk Help](https://helpcenter.spendesk.com/en/articles/11739382-anomaly-and-fraud-checks-on-receipts-in-prepare)
- **Expensify:** restricted line items (alcohol, gambling, tobacco) are flagged from the receipt. — [Expensify Help: Workspace Rules](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules)
- Third-party (not a vendor feature): a blog example of flagging $5,000.00 from a supplier whose invoices usually look like $4,317.28 as unusual. — [Sagnik Bhattacharya blog](https://sagnikbhattacharya.com/blog/accountants-use-ai-spot-errors-client-books)

### Inferences
- The duplicate key used is consistently contact/supplier + date + amount (Dext Precision), with cross-employee scope at the enterprise end (Concur).
- "Unusual vs history" is a premium-tier feature everywhere it appears: QBO Plus/Advanced, Concur Detect as an add-on, Sage Intacct (mid-market). It is not part of basic auto-coding.
- Dismissing a flag, as in Dext Precision, makes the system stop counting that combination. That suppression memory is a design element worth copying.

### Gaps
- No vendor documents the statistical method or multiplier (for example "2x supplier average") behind its unusual-amount flags.
- Xero has no documented native anomaly flag for unusual amounts in reconciliation.

## Q4. Ranking or prioritising review items by risk or value

### Takeaway
Explicit risk ranking is documented only at the enterprise expense end: Concur Detect prioritises "based on confidence and potential impact", and Brex says its audit agent groups violations by risk level. Most others *bucket* instead of rank: Ramp's ready-to-approve / to-review / ready-to-reject, Pleo's OK / flagged / Unknown, QBO's Business Feed "daily checklist", and Xero's Reconciled vs needs-attention.

### Cited Findings
- Concur Detect: risk findings "prioritize exceptions based on confidence and potential impact". Customers auto-approve low-risk reports so auditors focus on medium and high risk. — [Concur: Detect by Oversight](https://www.concur.com.au/detect-expense-fraud); [Concur Community user post](https://community.concur.com/t5/Additional-Products-Forum/How-are-users-liking-Concur-Detect/m-p/6546)
- Brex (marketing): the review agent auto-approves low-risk expenses and requests, escalates exceptions, and follows up with non-compliant users. The audit agent "categorizes potential violations by risk level". — [Brex: Agents on Brex](https://www.brex.com/platform/brex-ai); [Brex: Expense management](https://www.brex.com/product/expense-management)
- Unverified third-party claim: Brex's agent handles 99% of expense reports without human involvement. No Brex source was found. — [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/brexs-ai-agent-handles-99-of-expense-reports-without-human-intervention-and-the-implications-are-staggering/)
- Ramp: the homepage groups recommendations into "ready to approve", "to review" and "ready to reject" sections, and each expense has an activity feed with the rationale. Admins can require human review for all Requires-review and Rejection-recommended items. — [Ramp Support: Policy Agent Overview](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/44072387128979-Policy-Agent-Overview); [Ramp Support: Use Policy Agent for approvals](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/47618318137875-Use-Policy-Agent-for-approvals)
- QBO: the Business Feed consolidates items needing attention into a "daily financial checklist". No ranking is described. — [QuickBooks: Accounting agent](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/global/resources/product-update/quickbooks-accounting-agent/)
- QBO Accountant Books review: the Transaction review tab flags uncategorized transactions, transactions without payees, and unapplied payments. — [QuickBooks Help: Month-end reviews](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/manage-client/month-end-reviews-finish-clients-open-tasks/L4GubaqKy_US_en_US)
- Expensify: violations route the expense to manual approval. A 5% random sample of *compliant* reports also goes to a human. — [Expensify: Random report audit](https://help.expensify.com/articles/expensify-classic/reports/Set-a-random-report-audit-schedule)
- Dext Precision "Focus": a custom list of checks assigned to a team member to work through. — [Dext Help (Precision)](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/duplicate-transactions) (from search summary)

### Inferences
- Value-based prioritisation (sort by £ impact) is described explicitly only by Concur Detect ("potential impact"). For SMB accounting tools, a materiality-weighted queue would go beyond what mainstream products document.
- Random sampling of "clean" items (Expensify's 5%) is the only documented guard against silent errors in the auto-approved stream.

### Gaps
- No documentation on how Concur Detect or Brex compute risk level.
- No documentation of value-weighted sorting in Xero, QBO, Dext or Pleo.

## Q5. Learning from accountant corrections

### Takeaway
There are two layers everywhere. Explicit rules (Xero bank rules, Dext / AutoEntry / Hubdoc supplier rules, Expensify merchant defaults) are deterministic and take precedence. Implicit "memory" or ML from past coding fills in when no rule fires (Xero Memory/Prediction, QBO acceptance history, AutoEntry reuse of a supplier's coding). Some vendors promote memory into rules or automation: Dext suggests suppliers "ready for auto-publish" based on edit-free history, and QBO only turns a match green after repeated acceptances. AI reviewers (Ramp, Brex) collect thumbs-up/down feedback, but the docs say it feeds Ramp's model and system improvements and policy-editor insights, not per-customer auto-rules.

### Cited Findings
- **Xero:** suggestions come, in order, from matching an existing transaction, bank rules, "memory from similar reconciled transactions", and, if none apply, ML prediction. Memory/prediction can be turned off with the "Suggest previous entries" checkbox. — [Xero Product Ideas: Bank reconciliation, disable 'Suggest previous entries'](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967121-users-setup/suggestions/49510907-bank-reconciliation-add-setting-to-disable-sugg)
- Third-party guidance: bank rules are evaluated in display order, first match wins. Memory suggestions show in italics. — [More Than Accountants: Cash coding](https://morethanaccountants.co.uk/?p=2535); [Woodard: Xero and QuickBooks bank coding differences](https://report.woodard.com/articles/xero-and-quickbooks-bank-coding-differences-ocawr)
- **Xero JAX** uses patterns from the organisation's own reconciliation history plus predictions from similar anonymised transactions across Xero, and "learns from your actions". The mechanism is not specified. — [Xero Blog](https://blog.xero.com/product-updates/automatic-bank-reconciliation-jax-beta/)
- **Xero "Find & Re-code"** lets accountants bulk-search and correct coding. — [Accountants Daily](https://accountantsdaily.com.au/business/8147-xero-releases-new-re-code-feature)
- **QBO:** changing a suggested category means "QuickBooks will learn for next time". Filling in From/To improves future suggestions, and match confidence rises with repeated acceptances of that match type. — [QuickBooks Help](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-global/help-article/bank-transactions/ai-suggestions-help-match-categorise-bank/L8FHOh4AD_ROW_en)
- **Dext supplier/customer rules** set a default category, due date and tax rate for future documents from that supplier. Rules run after extraction. — [Dext Help: How to use supplier and customer rules](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/how-to-use-supplier-and-customer-rules); [Dext Help: Rules and automation](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/rules-and-automation-in-dext)
- **Dext** shows suppliers that are "ready for auto-publish", based on items published *without manual edits* over the past three months. Suggestions are opt-in and need the Advanced plan. — [Dext blog: Everyday automation wins](https://dext.com/au/blog/single/do-more-with-dext-guide-to-everyday-automation-wins); [Dext Help: How to use auto-publish](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/377051-how-to-use-auto-publish-in-dext) (the three-month detail comes from a search summary; not re-verified on the fetched page)
- **AutoEntry** suggests coding for new suppliers. Once a coding is chosen, it autofills other inbox invoices from that supplier and uses it as the default going forward. Sage markets that AutoEntry "learns how you analyse and process invoices and receipts like accounts, nominal, and tax codes". — [AutoEntry Help: Selecting supplier account, category and tax codes](https://intercom.help/autoentry/en/articles/1312846-selecting-supplier-account-category-and-tax-codes); [Sage: AutoEntry](https://www.sage.com/en-gb/accountants/products/autoentry/)
- **Hubdoc** supplier "configurations" decide how documents publish. Third-party guides advise confirming extraction accuracy for a supplier before enabling auto-sync. — [Hubdoc: Configurations explained](https://support.hubdoc.com/hc/en-us/articles/16659762446605-Configurations-explained); [Fastlane: Xero Hubdoc integration](https://fastlane-global.com/blog/xero-hubdoc-integration/)
- **Expensify** expense defaults auto-fill category, tag, description or tax rate by merchant or category, plus MCC-based default categories. Concierge can draft a rule from plain English, and nothing is saved until the admin confirms. — [Expensify Help: Workspace Rules](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules)
- **Ramp:** thumbs-up/down and reviewer overrides are logged in the audit log and "sent back to Ramp for model and system improvements". They also inform insights in the policy editor; in-editor policy suggestions are "coming soon". — [Ramp Support: Policy Agent Overview](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/44072387128979-Policy-Agent-Overview)
- **Brex:** agents are "trained on your policy and SOPs", and admins can review agent actions and give feedback. — [Brex: Agents on Brex](https://www.brex.com/platform/brex-ai)
- **Pleo:** automatic categorisation from past behaviour is listed as "coming soon" on its AI page (status unclear). Pleo's blog advises treating AI categorisation as a draft. — [Pleo: Artificial intelligence](https://www.pleo.io/en/artificial-intelligence); [Pleo blog: AI for spending controls](https://blog.pleo.io/en/ai-for-spending-controls)

### Inferences
- Dext's "ready for auto-publish" signal is the clearest documented example of promoting from human-reviewed to automatic based on an observed track record. QBO's "green only after several acceptances" is the same idea at the suggestion level.
- Explicit rules beat learned memory in every product. Learned behaviour is a fallback, and in Xero it can be switched off.
- None of the expense AI reviewers documents per-customer learning that changes future verdicts automatically. Feedback goes into vendor model improvement or into suggestions for policy edits, which keeps the written policy as the source of truth.

### Gaps
- No vendor explains how corrections are weighted, or whether one correction overrides a long history.
- Whether Xero JAX auto-creates bank rules from repeated corrections: not found.

## Q6. Collecting missing information from the employee or client

### Takeaway
There are three channels. (1) **At submission:** mandatory fields per category (Expensify: description, attendees, receipt; Brex: meal attendees), pre-filled where possible (Brex from calendar and L3 card data; Ramp memos from SMS replies). (2) **Push nudges:** SMS / email / in-app comments per transaction, with escalation and card locks (Ramp, Brex, Spendesk bulk reminders, Pleo per-user reminders). (3) **Batched accountant-to-client request lists** in bookkeeping tools: QBO "My accountant > Requests", Xero HQ "Ask" queries, and Dext item messages and paperwork requests through its mobile app. Third-party tools (Client Hub, Financial Cents, Uncat, Double) exist because native batching is limited.

### Cited Findings
**Accountant to client (bookkeeping)**
- **QBO Accountant:** from Books review, the accountant shares transactions with the client. The client gets an email, then answers in their own file under My accountant > Requests ("explain what they bought and why"). Replies show under Client requests > View all requests. The Transaction review tab pre-flags uncategorized transactions, those without payees, and unapplied payments. Caveat: requests due in a future year don't appear in Books review. — [QuickBooks Help: Month-end reviews](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/manage-client/month-end-reviews-finish-clients-open-tasks/L4GubaqKy_US_en_US); [QuickBooks Community (UK)](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-uk/manage-clients/do-month-end-reviews-to-finish-your-clients-open-tasks/01/784319)
- **QBO Collaboration (Beta, Essentials/Plus/Advanced):** "limited availability and subject to change". — [QuickBooks: Accounting agent](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/global/resources/product-update/quickbooks-accounting-agent/)
- **Xero HQ "Ask":** the accountant creates queries and sends them to the client's email, even if the client has no Xero organisation. The client replies through a secure email login, and outstanding queries are tracked. Workpapers has its own queries feature. A product-ideas request for an "Advisors Query Tag" on reconciled transactions suggests there is no native per-transaction query flag. — [Xero HQ](https://www.xero.com/nz/features-and-tools/practice-tools/xero-hq/); [Xero Product Ideas: Advisors Query Tag](https://productideas.xero.com/forums/940636-for-accountants-bookkeepers/suggestions/50123670--advisors-query-tag-for-reconciled-transactions)
- **Dext item messaging:** accountants send a direct message about a specific item to a client on the mobile app, and the client replies in-app. — [Dext Help: Item messaging](https://help.dext.com/en/s/article/item-messaging)
- **Dext paperwork requests for bank transactions:** sent from the web app to the client's mobile app (Notifications > Paperwork Requests). Answered requests are marked answered and Dext tries to match the document to the bank line. The client must have the mobile app to appear in the list. — [Dext Help: Requesting paperwork for bank transactions](https://help.dext.com/en/articles/416736-requesting-paperwork-for-bank-transactions)
- **Third-party add-ons for uncategorized-transaction client questions:** Client Hub (QBO/Xero; answers can update the transaction and sync attachments), Financial Cents "ReCats", Uncat. In Double, client contacts are *not* notified when a transaction question is asked. — [Client Hub: QBO uncategorized](https://www.clienthub.app/blog/client-hub-integration-with-quickbooks-resolving-uncategorized-expenses); [Financial Cents: ReCats](https://financial-cents.com/resources/articles/introducing-recats-manage-uncategorized-transactions/); [Double Help: Transaction questions](https://help.doublehq.com/en/articles/6326997-transaction-questions)

**Employee (expense management)**
- **Ramp:** texts the cardholder when an in-person purchase needs a receipt. The cardholder replies with a photo, and any text becomes the memo. Replying "OOPS" starts a missing-receipt policy exception. There are automatic SMS and email reminders, manual admin reminders by email listing the transactions, escalation to managers (per a Ramp blog; marketing), and optional card auto-lock for overdue items. Policy Agent sets "Requires review" when context is missing and re-assesses when a receipt or memo is added. — [Ramp Support: Submitting receipts, memos and accounting](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042588454-Submitting-receipts-memos-and-accounting-for-your-Ramp-transactions); [Ramp Support: What to do if you're missing a receipt](https://support.ramp.com/what-to-do-if-youre-missing-a-receipt); [Ramp Support: Missing-items auto-locks](https://support.ramp.com/missing-items-auto-locks); [Ramp blog: automated reminders](https://ramp.com/blog/receipt-compliance/send-automated-reminders-when-receipts-are-overdue); [Ramp Support: Policy Agent Overview](https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/44072387128979-Policy-Agent-Overview)
- **Brex Assistant:** "auto-pilot" is on by default and fills receipts, memos, and internal/external attendees "as requested by policy". It reads calendar events only around the time of the expense. It comments on policy-violating expenses to request more information. AI messages carry a diamond symbol, and Brex warns that suggestions may be inaccurate. Admins and employees can turn auto-pilot off. Receipts can be auto-generated from enhanced card data for supported merchants. — [Brex Support: Brex Assistant](https://brex.com/support/brex-assistant); [Brex: Expense management](https://www.brex.com/product/expense-management); [PR Newswire: Brex gives every employee an AI expense assistant (2023)](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/brex-gives-every-employee-an-expense-assistant-with-ai-301931043.html)
- **Expensify:** mandatory fields per category (description, attendees, receipt, itemized receipt) are enforced at submission as violations. Reports with Concierge audit alerts must have the alerts reviewed and accepted before approval (Control plan). — [Expensify Help: Workspace Rules](https://help.expensify.com/articles/new-expensify/workspaces/Workspace-Rules); [Expensify Community: Concierge Audit deep dive](https://community.expensify.com/discussion/5581/src)
- **Spendesk (July 2025):** one-click bulk reminders for missing receipts, and draft expense claims employees can finish later. Failed receipt checks call for contacting the employee for the correct receipt. — [Spendesk: Summer updates 2025](https://www.spendesk.com/platform/platform-releases/summer-updates-2025/); [Spendesk Help](https://helpcenter.spendesk.com/en/articles/11739382-anomaly-and-fraud-checks-on-receipts-in-prepare)
- **Pleo:** receipt reminders are a per-user setting. "Fetch" pulls receipts from email and matches them to expenses. — [Pleo Help: Finance automation](https://pleohelp.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/103000398332-finance-automation-with-pleo) (search snippet)

### Inferences
- Expense tools ask the *person who spent*, immediately and per transaction, by SMS or in-app, often with pre-filled drafts. Accountant tools ask the *client*, later and in batches, by email or portal. An AI accountant serving SMB clients sits between the two. Batching queries per client per period (as QBO and Xero HQ do), while pre-filling likely answers (as Brex does), is a combination no single mainstream bookkeeping product documents.
- Pre-filling from context (calendar for attendees, card L3 data for receipts, an SMS body as the memo) is the expense vendors' main way to cut questions.
- Hard enforcement (card lock, can't submit) exists only where the vendor controls the card. Bookkeeping tools can only ask.

### Gaps
- No documentation found on whether Xero JAX or QBO's agent *generates* client questions automatically from low-confidence items. Both appear to queue them for the accountant, not the client.
- Sage Copilot client-query features: nothing found.
- Expensify per-diem handling and Concierge's conversational follow-ups with employees were not documented in what was retrieved.
